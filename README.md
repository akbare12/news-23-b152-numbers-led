One image summing up 23 November 2026's world news: the AI rout that hit Asian
markets (Kospi −3.3%), Guterres' call to regulate AI before it's too late, and the
US lead over China falling to a record low in the Asia Power Index — plus 28 more
stories, a world map with 13 pins, and a date check separating 23 November from
earlier days.

Built with Python + Pillow (`draw.py`); `main.py` is stdlib-only and verifies the
PNG before writing it to `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`.
